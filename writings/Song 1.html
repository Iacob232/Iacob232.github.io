Song 1

ten years ago
i found out that the key to life
was a frantic disposition
and a glass of wine to go with it
in the intervening years
i've become a bit unsure
of how frantic i should be at times
and how tall a glass to bring with me

five years from now
i see myself deep in the earth
digging trenches for the mafia
with my muddy glass of wine along
when i'm through and done with it
i hope the boss will give
a life and fife of bliss to me
regardless of what's in his glass

and when i'm gone
some ninety something years from now
someone will take my glass of wine
and taste it for the final time
and when i awake right there
i'll make them swear to cherish life
by filling it with franticness
even if they hate its provenance

and only then they'll come to see:
the key to life's not on tv!
