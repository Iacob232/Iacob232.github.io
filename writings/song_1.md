ten years ago<br>
i found out that the key to life<br>
was a frantic disposition<br>
and a glass of wine to go with it<br>
in the intervening years<br>
i've become a bit unsure<br>
of how frantic i should be at times<br>
and how tall a glass to bring with me<br>

five years from now<br>
i see myself deep in the earth<br>
digging trenches for the mafia<br>
with my muddy glass of wine along<br>
when i'm through and done with it<br>
i hope the boss will give<br>
a life of strife and bliss to me<br>
regardless of what's in his glass<br>

and when i'm gone<br>
some ninety something years from now<br>
someone will take my glass of wine<br>
and taste it for the final time<br>
and when i awake right there<br>
i'll make them swear to cherish life<br>
by filling it with franticness<br>
even if they hate its provenance<br>

and only then they'll come to see:<br>
the key to life's not on tv!
