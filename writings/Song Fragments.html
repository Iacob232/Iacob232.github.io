Fragment 2

“Harana”

you would have sang to me on your guitar
and put a flower in my ear
instead
you showed me your crypto on your laptop
and put a knife in my neck

Fragment 4

“A Toss”

he plays his games and spends no time on me.
i heard him whisper something in his mic.
the only game he’ll play with me tonight
is finding his computer in the sea.

Fragment 5

“AABABA”

i can hear you through the wind
it whispers more than words aloud
and though it couldn’t tell me where
i won’t stop listening ‘til the day you’re found

though it doesn’t feel too soon
the months and years have passed you by
and still the clouds murmur your name
until you see the sun in all its light
