Song 25

those eyes keep me awake at night
what do i see in them?
there’s nothing left about his face
disfigured by those men

how could somebody see a child
no older than their son
and curse and yell and point at them
their military gun?

what could i’ve done to save his life?
i wasn’t in the town
to stop the bullets from his head
now lying on the ground

when we were only ten years old
and running in the park
he said “i want to change the world
i know i’ll leave my mark”

now i will have to live with him
no longer as a friend
but as a pamphlet on the street
his life shall never end
