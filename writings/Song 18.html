Song 18 (fragment)

out in these streets i
feel so alone
in bleak midwinter
frost on the stones

no roof above me
ice in the air
hems in my sweater
starting to tear

now in december
over the sea
i feel the mountains
call out to me

mountains so distant
i won’t arrive
if i can make it
i’ll feel alive

walking so thinly
what have i found
i’m in awe of the
moon on the ground

lunar reflections
bring me respite
i think i’ll make it
i’ll be alright
