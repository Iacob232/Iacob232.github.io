### “Urban Bucolic”
A word can change a life. I heard a song<br>
sung by a street performer to a bird<br>
that flew around his head as on he strummed<br>
about the end of times. “Do not delay!<br>
The world is filled with sickness and decay!<br>
And nobody will save you from that day<br>
unless you stop and pray.” The pigeon cooed<br>
unmoved by the invective. People shrugged,<br>
covered their ears, or shuffled through the plaza,<br>
determined to escape the siren song.<br>

The plaza was vacated, save the bird,<br>
myself, and the musician. On he strummed;<br>
his lyrics lost their meaning, and his voice<br>
was buried in the silence of the noise<br>
that emanated from the rattling strings<br>
until it resonated on these words:<br>

“Fɪʀᴇ! Dᴇʟᴜɢᴇ! Oᴄᴇᴀɴꜱ ꜰɪʟʟᴇᴅ ᴡɪᴛʜ ᴠᴇɴᴏᴍ!<br>
Bᴇɢ ꜰᴏʀ ᴍᴇʀᴄʏ! Rᴜɴ! Hɪᴅᴇ! Cʜᴀɴɢᴇ. Hᴇ’ꜱ ᴄᴏᴍɪɴɢ.”<br>

I woke. The pigeon fled. The plaza filled.<br>
The old guitarist vanished into air<br>
as did his voice. He left a plectrum there.<br>
Just as I read the word he scrawled on it,<br>
I heard the rouse.<br>
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;It was too late to change.
