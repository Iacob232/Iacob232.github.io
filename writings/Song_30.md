### “I’ll put you in my landscape”
I’ll put you in my landscape:<br>
in amidst a snow-covered forest<br>
where a lone lowing deer tries<br>
freeing its antler from a bush<br>
while you stand in wait nearby<br>
half hidden behind an arching yew<br>
with your spear grasped in your hand<br>
and a greyhound at your feet;<br>
but you pay no heed to it—<br>
something else catches your eye:<br>
your pupils grow<br>
your mouth quavers<br>
you grasp your spear<br>
firmer still;<br>
perhaps some better quarry<br>
not in the forest or the snow;<br>
something— something.<br>
Staring out ashamed that I still see you<br>
—there I will place you.
