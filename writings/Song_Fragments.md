## Fragment 2
### “Harana”
in another life<br>
you would have sang to me on your guitar<br>
and put a flower in my ear<br>
instead<br>
you showed me your crypto on your laptop<br>
and put a knife in my neck

## Fragment 4
### “A Toss”
he plays his games and spends no time on me.<br>
i heard him whisper something in his mic.<br>
the only game he’ll play with me tonight<br>
is finding his computer in the sea.

## Fragment 5
### “AABABA”
i can hear you through the wind<br>
it whispers more than words aloud<br>
and though it couldn’t tell me where<br>
i won’t stop listening ‘til the day you’re found<br><br>

though it doesn’t feel too soon<br>
the months and years have passed you by<br>
and still the clouds murmur your name<br>
until you see the sun in all its light
