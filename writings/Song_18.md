out in these streets i<br>
feel so alone<br>
in bleak midwinter<br>
frost on the stones<br><br>
no roof above me<br>
ice in the air<br>
hems in my sweatshirt<br>
starting to tear<br><br>
now in december<br>
over the sea<br>
i feel the mountains<br>
call out to me<br><br>
my legs are weary<br>
all i have left<br>
cold bed below me<br>
shortness of breath<br><br>
mountains so distant<br>
i won’t arrive<br>
if i can make it<br>
i’ll feel alive<br><br>
walking so thinly<br>
what have i found<br>
i’m in awe of the<br>
moon on the ground<br><br>
lunar reflections<br>
bring me respite<br>
i think i’ll make it<br>
i’ll be alright
