those eyes keep me awake at night<br>
what do i see in them?<br>
there’s nothing left about his face<br>
disfigured by those men<br><br>
how could somebody see a child<br>
no older than their son<br>
and curse and yell and point at them<br>
their military gun?<br><br>
what could i’ve done to save his life?<br>
i wasn’t in the town<br>
to stop the bullets from his head<br>
now lying on the ground<br><br>
when we were only ten years old<br>
and running in the park<br>
he said “i want to change the world<br>
i know i’ll leave my mark”<br><br>
now i will have to live with him<br>
no longer as a friend<br>
but as a pamphlet on the street<br>
his life shall never end
