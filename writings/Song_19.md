### “An Odd Occurrence in a Dream”
there was an old house far from the present<br>
and the one thing that i could recall<br>
was when i sat on the staircase deep in my thought<br>
i told myself to stare at the wall<br>
for the very next day they would paint it all white<br>
so i looked at it all through the night<br>
but the pattern’s lost to time now<br>

there was a red mandolin from east perth<br>
and the one thing i heard it play<br>
was rustican vamp in the key of b-flat<br>
and it went on for all of the day<br>
then the old luthier said “can you play it alright?”<br>
so i plucked it with all of my might<br>
but the melody’s lost to time now<br>

there was a large cat that laid on my stomach<br>
and couldn’t stop purring at me<br>
when i tried politely to shoo it away<br>
it licked me and slept at my feet<br>
i tried to get up but the cat weighed me down<br>
and i felt i was starting to drown<br>

one day i’ll be lost to time<br>
but i know who’ll be waiting for me
